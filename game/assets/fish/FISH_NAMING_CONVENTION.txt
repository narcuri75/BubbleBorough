Fish sprite-sheet naming convention
<fish-id>__genetics-<natural|enhanced>[__state-<state>].webp
<fish-id>__genetics-<natural|enhanced>[__state-<state>].json

Rules:
- WEBP and coordinate JSON always share the exact same stem.
- All fish live in the same fish folder.
- genetics-natural and genetics-enhanced are explicit and sortable.
- Special whole-sheet states use __state-..., such as pufferfish inflated.
- Variant coordinates and virtual sprite names stay inside the sidecar JSON.
- fish-types.json no longer contains the unused theme/habitat field.
- fish-types.json uses genetics: natural|enhanced for its catalog entries.
