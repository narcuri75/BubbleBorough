Hunger

Basics:

  • 🍗 Feed hungry fish before they start starving.
  • 💊 Add drops of medication to the tank when they are ill.
  • 🧽 Scrub the tank with the sponge to clean it.
  • 🥄 Scoop fish into storage if needed.
  • 🐟 Move fish between tank and storage.
  • 🌿 Move decor between tank and storage.
  • 🎨 Customize your tank.
  • 🛒 Buy fish, decor, customizations, and equipment.
  • ⚙️ Change Settings, Export/Import save data, and Reset game data.

Tips:

  • 💔 Starving fish can lose health.
  • 💖 Food restores hunger and comfort. Use medicine to restore lost health.
  • 💩 Fish poop 1 to 3 hours after eating and it settles on the gravel.
  • 🏚️ Bigger fish and more fish make grime build up faster.
  • 🐠 Sucker fish help slow grime build-up.

Coins:

  • 🪙 Each fish's first rewarded feeding of the day earns its listed Feeding Care value, up to 8 coins total per day.
  • 🧽 Cleaning at 25% grime or higher earns coins. Dirtier tanks pay more.
  • ☀️ Daily recaps and milestones award coins automatically.
  • 🪨 Fish may find coins in the gravel, and eligible fish or tanks can be sold.

Our Mission

To reshape biological life for a world that can no longer wait for nature to adapt on its own.

PROTEUS BIODYNE exists to expand the limits of biological adaptation.

We believe biology can do more.

The natural world is changing faster than many species can survive. Habitats are shrinking. Ecosystems are destabilizing. Human needs continue to grow. Evolution, once measured across thousands of generations, no longer operates on a timeline compatible with the challenges ahead.

Through advanced genetic engineering, directed adaptation, behavioral preservation, and controlled biological scaling, PROTEUS BIODYNE develops organisms capable of thriving under conditions nature never prepared them for.

Our work is designed to preserve biological potential, improve compatibility between species and human environments, and ensure that valuable life does not disappear simply because the world changed first.

Identity / PB-02

Why PROTEUS BIODYNE

Proteus, the ancient sea god of transformation, represents life’s ability to change form in response to its environment.

Biodyne combines biology with the concept of applied force, reflecting our belief that adaptation does not need to remain passive.

Together, the name represents our founding principle: biological change can be understood, directed, and engineered with purpose.

Adaptive Biology. Engineered.

Applied Research / PB-03

Controlled Biological Scaling

Miniaturized marine organisms are not novelties. They demonstrate that body scale can be radically altered without sacrificing cognitive, behavioral, or predatory phenotype integrity.

By preserving the defining capabilities of an organism while reducing the resources and habitat it requires, biological scaling creates new pathways for species survival, research, and integration.

Founding Principle / Internal Doctrine If an organism cannot survive the world we are creating, change the organism.

Lifecycle Recovery / PB-LR1Free for customers

Customer Stewardship Program

Return life to the cycle.

Proteus accepts deceased aquatic specimens at no cost to the owner. Every recovery is handled through our secure biological materials program, where viable tissue and biological records may support future conservation, research, and adaptation work.

By enrolling, owners authorize Proteus Biodyne to retain, process, study, or repurpose recovered specimens at its discretion. Specific downstream uses are confidential and are not individually disclosed.
