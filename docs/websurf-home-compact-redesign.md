# WebSurf Home compact redesign

Phase 9 converts the WebSurf home page from a landing-page layout into a compact browser dashboard.

- The welcome area is now a short account header with only the WebSurf brand, player welcome, and WebSurf email address.
- Favorites and Recent Pages are thin utility rows with content-width controls.
- Saved bookmark drag ordering from Phase 8 remains intact.
- The Inbox fills the remaining page height and its message list scrolls internally when needed.
- The WebSurf security/version and mail-storage readout are now the Inbox footer instead of a separate card.
- Home-page surfaces, text, borders, rows, buttons, and footer use the existing WebSurf light/dark theme variables.
- Decorative shadows and hover movement were removed from the Home dashboard in favor of restrained borders and compact spacing.
