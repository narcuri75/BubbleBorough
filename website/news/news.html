Beta V 0.2.2 — 10/2/2026 2:14PM — Update Notes

10/2/2026 2:14PM

This is the first official noted push for Bubble Borough.

Recent additions

  • Updated turn animation to give fish a more three-dimensional perspective.
  • Updated the fish leveling and variant unlock system.
  • Improved fish movement, behavior, and depth rendering.
  • Expanded decor interactions and aquarium customization.

Known issues

  • Fish sometimes get caught on decor or other fish, resulting in strange stuttering behavior.
  • When changing gravel color, the zoom-out frame may get stuck when exiting edit mode and only clear after refreshing the browser.
  • Fish can snap in a direction instead of gradually swimming that way.
  • Fish can disappear momentarily or clip through cave images and cave walls.
